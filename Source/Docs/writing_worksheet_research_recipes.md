# Research and recipe labels and descriptions
Grouped by drug chain. Includes the research tab, research projects, and crafting recipes.

## Shared

- **RP_ResearchTab** (research tab)
- - **label:** psychedelics

## Dried Mindcap

- **RP_MindcapPreparation** (research, neolithic, 600 cost, no prereqs)
- - **label:** mindcap preparation
- - **description:** Study the mindcap mushroom and develop a method for drying the raw flesh to stabilize its psychoactive compounds. This simple preparation technique requires no advanced equipment — just patience, heat, and a steady hand.

- **RP_Make_DriedMindcap** (recipe, campfire or drug lab, 3 mushrooms → 1 dried mindcap)
- - **label:** dry mindcap mushrooms
- - **description:** Carefully dry raw mindcap mushrooms to stabilize the psychoactive alkaloids. The drying process concentrates the compounds into a usable form.
- - **jobString:** Drying mindcap mushrooms.

## LYS

- **RP_LYSSynthesis** (research, industrial, 4000 cost, requires DrugProduction)
- - **label:** LYS synthesis
- - **description:** Develop the chemical process for synthesizing LYS from ergot alkaloids and neutroamine. The procedure is delicate and demanding, requiring a skilled chemist and a properly equipped drug lab. The result is the most potent psychedelic known to the Rim.

- **RP_Make_LYS** (recipe, drug lab, crafting 8 + intellectual 12, 100 ergo + 1 neutroamine → 10 LYS)
- - **label:** synthesize LYS
- - **description:** Synthesize LYS from ergot alkaloids and neutroamine through a precise chemical process. Requires advanced knowledge and careful lab work.
- - **jobString:** Synthesizing LYS.

## Fluff

- **RP_FluffSynthesis** (research, industrial, 1000 cost, requires DrugProduction)
- - **label:** fluff synthesis
- - **description:** Learn to convert safrole oil into fluff, a gentle empathogenic psychedelic. The chemical process is straightforward compared to LYS synthesis, requiring only basic intellectual aptitude and a drug lab.

- **RP_Make_Fluff** (recipe, drug lab, intellectual 4, 2 safrole → 1 fluff)
- - **label:** synthesize fluff
- - **description:** Process safrole oil into fluff through a relatively simple chemical reaction. Requires basic chemical knowledge.
- - **jobString:** Synthesizing fluff.
