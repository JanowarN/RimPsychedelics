# ThingDef labels and descriptions
Grouped by resource chain, from source plant through final drug product.

## Dried Mindcap

- **RP_Plant_MindcapMushroom** (plant, sow/harvest, grows in darkness)
- - **label:** mindcap mushroom
- - **description:** An unassuming campanulate-capped mushroom that grows in clusteres, the mindcap is a natural source of potent psychoactive alkaloids. Raw consumption causes severe sickness in both humans and animals, but when carefully dried, toxins break down and the alkaloids stabilize into a powerful psychedelic.\n\nMindcap mushrooms require darkness to grow and will die if exposed to sustained light.

- **RP_MindcapMushroom** (resource, rottable 10 days, 3 → 1 dried mindcap)
- - **label:** raw mindcap
- - **description:** Freshly harvested mindcap mushroom. The raw flesh is saturated with unstable compounds that cause violent sickness when consumed. The mushrooms must be carefully dried before they can be consumed safely.

- **RP_DriedMindcap** (drug, neolithic tech)
- - **label:** dried mindcap
- - **description:** A dried mushroom with blue veins and purple tinted flesh used as a powerful psychedelic across many worlds by people without the means to access more designer substances, or by those looking for a more "authentic" natural experience. Mindcap trips can be shorter and rougher around the edges, but are no less intense than more refined psychedelics like LYS. Subjective effects include open and closed-eye visuals, altered preception, enhanced introspection, and overall intense experiences. Trace toxins not completely broken down during the drying process tend to cause nausea.

## LYS

- **RP_Plant_Ergo** (plant, sow/harvest, fertility-sensitive, skill 6)
- - **label:** ergo plant
- - **description:** A half fungus/half grain genetically engineered to produce Ergo, a fleshy fungal pod that can be refined into LYS using less sophisticated methods than total synthesis from scratch.

- **RP_Ergo** (resource, rottable 30 days, 100 + 1 neutroamine → 10 LYS)
- - **label:** ergo
- - **description:** Dark, wrinkled fungal pods harvested from the ergo plant. They contain the volatile organic compounds needed to produce LYS. Consuming the pods raw causes severe, incapacitating sickness.

- **RP_LYS** (drug, industrial tech)
- - **label:** LYS
- - **description:** A powerful psychedelic drug that was designed during the third greate wave of colonization along with the bioengineered plant ergo. An amalgam of multiple entheogens, subjective effects include open and closed-eye visuals, time distortion, enhanced introspection, ego loss, and euphoria. LYS is commonly reported to be able to evoke mystical-type experiences that can facilitate self-reflection and personal growth.

## Fluff

- **RP_Plant_TreeSassafras** (wild tree, not plantable, deciduous)
- - **label:** sassafras tree
- - **description:** A medium-sized deciduous hardwood tree that grows naturally in temperate and tropical forests. Its wood is strong and versitile, but most notably safrole oil can be extracted from it's roots after being cut down.

- **RP_ChoppedStumpSassafras** (stump, high deterioration, harvestable)
- - **label:** chopped sassafras stump
- - **description:** The fresh stump of a felled sassafras tree. If extracted quickly, can yield safrole oil from the roots. Safrole extraction renders any wood from this stump unusable as a building material.

- **RP_Safrole** (resource, rottable 30 days, 2 + 1 neutroamine → 1 fluff)
- - **label:** safrole
- - **description:** A pungent, oily extract from sassafras root bark. Has no nutritional value and smells like root beer left in the sun too long, but a key precursor in the synthesis of fluff.

- **RP_Fluff** (drug, industrial tech)
- - **label:** fluff
- - **description:** A psychedelic drug used recreationally for millenia, the basic chemistry has remained mostly unchanged since humanity first took to the stars. While not particularly hallucinogenic, inhibitions and trust in others are greatly impacted. Recreational users describe the experience as "Just good vibes, man. helluva hangover though."
